Privacy Policy

Effective November 22, 2026

The short version: Unfold UI does not collect your data, does not send it anywhere, and does not share it with anyone. The app has no internet access. Everything it uses to run your home screen stays on your phone.

What we collect

Nothing. Unfold UI does not have permission to use the internet, and it includes no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, and no accounts. There is no server to receive your data, so none of it leaves your device.

What stays on your device

To arrange your home screen, Unfold UI stores your layout, settings, folders, and favorites in the app's own private storage on your phone. If you create a backup, it is written to a file you choose and stays wherever you save it. We never receive any of this.

Permissions, and why the app asks

Unfold UI requests only what a launcher needs. Most permissions are optional and tied to a feature you turn on; you can grant or revoke each one in Android Settings at any time. The app works without the optional ones.

Installed appsBuilds your app drawer and home screen. Read on the device through Android's standard launcher interface; the app never requests the broad "all apps" permission, and this list is never sent anywhere.
Contacts (optional)Lets you find contacts from search or the Side Shelf. Asked only when you turn that feature on. Contacts are read on the device and are never stored by us or sent anywhere.
Usage access (optional)Shows recent or suggested apps (Quick Picks and the Taskbar). You enable it on a system Settings screen and can revoke it anytime.
Display over other apps (optional)Lets the Side Shelf and Taskbar float over the app you're using. Enabled by you in system Settings.
Notification access (optional)Shows unread badges on app icons. Enabled by you in system Settings; it reads notification counts to draw the badge and sends nothing.
Accessibility service (optional)Lets you double-tap an empty spot to lock the screen, and open two apps side by side. It reads nothing on your screen. You turn it on in Accessibility settings and can turn it off anytime.
Set wallpaperApplies a wallpaper you choose.
Fingerprint / face / PINUnlocks private folders, handled by Android's own biometric prompt. Unfold UI never sees your biometric data.

Sharing

We share nothing, because we collect nothing. There are no third-party analytics, advertising, or other components in the app that send your data out.

Children

Unfold UI is a general-audience app and is not directed to children. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data lives on your device. Uninstalling Unfold UI removes the app's data from your phone. If you have Android's system backup turned on, Android may copy app settings to your own Google account under Google's terms; that is a feature of your device and Google's service, not something we receive or control.

Security

Because your data never leaves your device, the main risk is someone with physical access to your unlocked phone. Keep a screen lock on your device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top. If a future version of the app ever collects or sends data, we will update this policy and the app's Google Play Data safety information before that version is released.
